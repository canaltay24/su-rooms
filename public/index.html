About Sabancı Room Finder

How does this app know room schedules?

Sabancı University publishes all semester course offerings, lecture times, recitations, and classroom allocations publicly on the Student Information System (SUIS / BannerWeb) at suis.sabanciuniv.edu.

Our scraper pulls all sections across every subject (CS, ENS, MATH, ACC, ECON, SPS, etc.) and parses the day of the week, start/end time, instructor, and classroom code (e.g., FENS G035, FMAN L018, FASS 1001, UC G030).

Building Code Reference

  • FENS: Faculty of Engineering and Natural Sciences (Mühendislik)
  • FMAN: Sabancı Business School (Yönetim Bilimleri)
  • FASS: Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences (Sanat & Sosyal Bilimler)
  • UC: University Center (Üniversite Merkezi)
  • SL: School of Languages (Diller Okulu)
  • SUSAM: Sakıp Sabancı Arts & Research Center

Floor Code Reference

Sabancı classroom numbering designates:

L = Lower Ground / Bodrum (-1) G = Ground / Zemin (0) 1 = 1st Floor (1xxx) 2 = 2nd Floor (2xxx)
